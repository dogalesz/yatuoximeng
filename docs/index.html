Current focus

llocus · Current work

Eye tracking,
without the eye tracker.

llocus removes the hardware requirement from eye tracking. RGB camera frames go in; a stable gaze signal comes out. Calibration, per-user adaptation, movement handling, and continual learning live inside the engine.

It runs locally on about one CPU core, keeps webcam video on the device, and is being built for desktop, embedded, industrial, accessibility, and research use.
