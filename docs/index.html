01 / About

Founder-engineer,
close to the work.

I’m interested in the space where ambitious products meet difficult engineering. I like understanding the whole system: the user problem, the product surface, and the machinery underneath it.

Today, that means building llocus — a company making computer interaction more natural on ordinary hardware. I’m also the person behind compiler, desktop, embedded, and web projects that I’ve built from the ground up.

Along the way, I’ve mentored several peers through their coding journeys. I care about making hard technical ideas legible, useful, and shippable.

Current focus

llocus · Founder & CEO

Making hands-free
computing practical.

llocus is building an eye-powered reading and navigation layer for students and people who spend long hours with documents, research, and the web.

The product is in private beta with 100+ pre-registrations. No inflated launch claims — just careful engineering and a clear path from prototype to product.
